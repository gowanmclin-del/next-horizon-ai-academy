# Next Horizon AI Academy — Glean Partner Follow-Up Brief

## 1. Company positioning
Next Horizon AI Academy is building an AI adoption, workforce enablement, and implementation practice that helps organizations move from AI interest to practical business use. We combine readiness assessment, employee and leadership enablement, use-case discovery, workflow design, implementation planning, adoption support, and outcome measurement.

Our role is to connect three parts of successful enterprise AI adoption:
1. People — workforce capability, confidence, responsible use, and change enablement.
2. Process — high-value use cases, workflows, human review, and measurable outcomes.
3. Technology — enterprise AI platforms, knowledge systems, copilots, agents, and workflow tools selected for the customer’s environment.

## 2. Next Horizon Corporate Partnership Program
The Corporate Partnership Program gives organizations several ways to engage with Next Horizon:
- Workforce Adoption Partner — employee AI readiness, training, leadership enablement, and change support.
- Implementation & Solutions Partner — use-case discovery, workflow design, pilots, implementation planning, and adoption optimization.
- Curriculum Licensing Partner — structured Next Horizon curriculum for organizational or workforce learning.
- Community & Strategic Partner — sponsored training, workforce programs, technology education, and broader AI-skilling initiatives.

Our delivery model follows four stages: Assess → Enable → Implement → Adopt & Optimize.

## 3. AI Workforce Readiness Assessment
The assessment provides a directional 0–100 view of organizational readiness across seven dimensions:
- Current use of approved AI tools
- Structured workforce training and enablement
- AI-use guidelines and human-review expectations
- Prompting and AI communication capability
- Verification practices
- Integration into repeatable workflows or business processes
- Leadership readiness to sponsor use cases and measure outcomes

The assessment is designed to start a deeper discovery conversation, not replace technical, security, legal, or governance review.

## 4. AI Foundations + Adoption Pilot
A focused pilot can begin with a small team and move through four phases:
1. Baseline — readiness assessment, stakeholder discovery, approved tools, risks, barriers, and goals.
2. Enable — AI foundations, prompting, verification, responsible-use, and role-specific learning.
3. Apply — identify priority use cases and map at least one reusable workflow, agent concept, or implementation candidate.
4. Roadmap — summarize results, adoption gaps, implementation recommendations, and next-step priorities.

Potential deliverables include readiness findings, Academy learning, workshop facilitation, use-case map, workflow design, completion assessment, certificates, adoption findings, and an implementation roadmap.

## 5. Example: How Glean could fit into a client workflow
### Client scenario
A 250-person professional-services organization stores knowledge across cloud drives, messaging platforms, project-management systems, CRM records, internal documentation, and other approved enterprise applications. Employees lose time searching for answers and repeatedly ask experienced staff for information that already exists somewhere inside the company.

### Business objective
Reduce time spent locating trusted internal knowledge while improving employee onboarding, consistency, and productivity.

### Next Horizon engagement
**Assess:** Interview stakeholders, review workforce readiness, map knowledge-search pain points, identify the highest-value employee questions, and establish baseline measures such as time-to-answer and user confidence.

**Enable:** Train employees and team champions on enterprise AI use, effective querying/prompting, verification, permissions awareness, and responsible use.

**Implement:** Working with the customer and appropriate technical resources, define the priority Glean use case, approved knowledge sources, target users, workflow, permissions expectations, success criteria, launch cohort, and any agent/workflow opportunities.

**Adopt:** Support launch communications, user onboarding, office hours, champion enablement, feedback collection, and workflow refinement.

**Optimize:** Review adoption, user feedback, answer quality, time saved, and new use cases. Build a roadmap for additional Glean agents, workflows, knowledge sources, departments, or employee experiences.

### Potential employee workflow
1. Employee has a client or operational question.
2. Glean retrieves relevant company knowledge from connected systems while respecting source permissions.
3. The employee receives a contextual answer grounded in authorized enterprise information.
4. Where appropriate, the answer supports a next action or Glean Agent/workflow.
5. The employee verifies high-impact outputs according to company policy.
6. Adoption and outcome data inform workflow refinement and expansion.

### Why Next Horizon fits
Next Horizon’s differentiator is the bridge between technology implementation and the people expected to use it. We are building the capability to help organizations identify useful AI applications, prepare employees, define workflows, support implementation, drive adoption, and measure whether the technology is producing practical value.
